its bin.bnbstatic.com CDN. sources.json records the exact asset codes, official
asset names and URLs, and SHA-256 checksums.

Currency-pair, commodity and generated fallback marks are original inline SVG
in src/symbol-mark.js. They do not claim to be official brand logos.
